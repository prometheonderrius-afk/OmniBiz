# Cold Outreach "Hook" Email Template

This is a highly optimized, 3-sentence cold email template designed to get replies from local roofing or HVAC business owners. D.J. can customize the brackets and send this to the qualified leads.

---

### Subject Options:
- Quick question about missed calls at [Business Name]
- Automated text-back demo for [Business Name]
- [City] [Roofing/HVAC] - lost leads check

### 3-Sentence Hook Email Body:

Hi [Owner Name],

I was looking at local [roofing/HVAC] companies in [City] and noticed that your website ([Website Link]) doesn't have an instant text option or web-chat. 

In local home services, over 60% of incoming calls go unanswered and customers immediately call the next contractor on Google, which means this simple gap is likely costing you several jobs a month. 

I built a quick prototype showing how we can automatically text back anyone who calls your business but doesn't get an answer; would you like me to send you a 1-minute demo video or a phone number to test it yourself?

Best regards,

D.J.
ElevateAI
